FanPilot: fine-grained fan control for Apple Silicon Macs (macOS 26 or later)

1. Drag FanPilot onto the Applications folder.

2. Open FanPilot from Applications.
   FanPilot isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first time macOS says it
   can't check it. Click Done, then:
   System Settings > Privacy & Security > scroll down to "FanPilot was
   blocked" > Open Anyway > confirm with your password.
   (Or, in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/FanPilot.app )

3. Allow the fan helper.
   FanPilot changes fan speed through a small helper that's built into the
   app. macOS asks you once to allow it: click "Open Login Items…" in
   FanPilot and turn FanPilot on under "Allow in the Background".

That's it. Pick a profile from the fan icon in the menu bar.

Undo: choose the macOS profile, or turn the helper off in FanPilot > Helper,
then move FanPilot to the Bin. The fans go back to macOS.

Source code, help and issues: https://github.com/antonarnaudov/FanPilot
